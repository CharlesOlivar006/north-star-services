Good to know

A few common questions.

How does the estimate calculator work?

It uses sample prices and simple multipliers based on your selections. A real business would need to confirm pricing, scope, and any extra fees.

Can I request more than one service?

This demo calculates one service at a time. A production version could support multiple selections and a combined estimate.

Are the prices and service locations real?

No. Northstar Home Services is a fictional portfolio concept. All prices, neighborhoods, and service descriptions are examples for demonstration.

Does the contact form send a booking?

No. The form currently demonstrates front-end validation only. It does not send messages, save information, or create real bookings.
